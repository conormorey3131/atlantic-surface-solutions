Protective Wax Systems

Bilt Hamber Wax Protection

We use premium Bilt Hamber corrosion protection products, selected for their proven performance and quality. The right products matter just as much as the preparation. Three stages of wax protection let us tailor the treatment to each vehicle.

The Three Stages

Protection tailored to the vehicle

01
Stage One

Cavity Wax Protection

Specialist cavity wax is injected into internal cavities, seams, sills and other enclosed sections of the vehicle, including areas that cannot be accessed through conventional application methods. The wax penetrates difficult-to-reach areas, helping to protect internal metal surfaces and seams against moisture and corrosion.

02
Stage Two

Heavy-Duty Underbody Wax

A heavy-duty protective wax applied to suitable underbody panels, wheel arches, bumper panels and other exposed areas. It leaves a characteristic brownish finish and forms a protective barrier against moisture, road salt, dirt and corrosion, particularly beneficial for vehicles exposed to harsh conditions, rough terrain and demanding working environments.

03
Stage Three

Clear Underbody & Suspension Protection

For customers who prefer a cleaner, more discreet appearance, a clear protective wax coating is applied to suitable underbody and suspension components. It provides corrosion protection while drying to a touch-dry finish, retaining the vehicle's original, factory-style appearance.

Choosing the right protection

Working Jeeps and 4x4s: heavy-duty underbody wax is particularly suitable for vehicles regularly driven on rough ground, construction sites, farms and demanding working environments.

Everyday vehicles and classic cars: clear underbody protection is an excellent option for daily drivers and light-use vehicles where maintaining a factory-style appearance is important.

We discuss the options with every customer before work begins, so the chosen protection suits the vehicle and its intended use.

Why we choose Bilt Hamber

We use Bilt Hamber products because of their reputation for quality, proven performance and suitability for professional corrosion protection. Their products have undergone manufacturer testing and are developed specifically to provide effective protection against corrosion.

Bilt Hamber is a respected UK brand, recognised for its research, product development and high-performance anti-corrosion technology.

Keeping It Protected

Periodic inspections & wax top-ups

Underbody protection requires ongoing maintenance. Exposure to water, road salt and road debris can gradually wear away or damage protective coatings, leaving metal surfaces vulnerable to corrosion.

We recommend periodic inspections to check the condition of your vehicle's underbody and suspension protection. Where necessary, we can carry out protective wax top-ups to restore coverage and maintain corrosion protection, helping to extend the life of the treatment.
