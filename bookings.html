Vehicle Collection & Delivery Service

For added convenience, we offer an optional vehicle collection and return service. This service is available at an additional cost depending on the collection and delivery distance, with pricing agreed in advance.

How long it takes

A full underbody restoration and protection treatment on a standard passenger car requires a minimum of 4 days. The timeframe varies with the size and condition of the vehicle, the extent of corrosion, the preparation required and the drying and curing times of the coatings. An estimated completion date is provided following the initial inspection.
